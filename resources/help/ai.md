# AI features and credits

The app has a few AI helpers: the writing assistant in the composer, alt text for images, idea generation, and the
shortener that fits a long caption into a network's limit. They share one monthly allowance of AI credits.

## AI credits

AI credits come with the Plan each calendar month. A Trial gets its own amount for the whole Trial. Members use the
Owner's credits, unused credits do not carry over, and the amount shows on Billing under Usage.

A call uses credits based on the work it does, and the balance updates after each call. When credits run out, the AI
buttons stop working until the next month. The Owner sees the renewal date and Change plan; a member sees the same
and is pointed to the workspace owner.

## What the helpers do

- **Writing assistant**: writes, rephrases, shortens, expands or changes the tone of a caption. It never posts by
  itself.
- **Alt text**: describes an image for accessibility. Edit the description before saving if it drifts.
- **Idea generation**: fills the ideas board with starting points from a short prompt.
- **Shortener**: trims a caption to fit a network's limit without cutting a word in half.

If a helper cannot answer, it shows a short error and spends no credits. Try again later.

## Privacy

The provider behind the AI features is not named in the app; it is a third-party AI service provider, as the
Privacy page says. The text you send to a helper is used only to produce its answer.
