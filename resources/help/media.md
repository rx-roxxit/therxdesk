# Media, storage and video policy

Media you upload counts toward the storage in your Plan. An Owner's storage is counted across every workspace they
own; a member's uploads count toward the same Owner.

## Uploading

Add images, videos or documents from the composer, or import them from a connected service. Each file must be
inside the network's own limits and inside the workspace's storage. If storage is full, uploads are refused until
space is freed or the Plan changes. Published posts are never refused for storage.

## Where files live

Files are stored outside the app server and served from a media address. Deleting a post removes its media from
storage.

## Video policy

Videos are large, so an Owner chooses what happens to a video after its post publishes:

- **Keep** leaves it in storage.
- **Delete** removes it once the post is published.
- **Backup** uploads it to the Owner's Google Drive, then removes it from our storage.

Change the policy under Settings, then Preferences, in the Video policy section. A failed backup is recorded on the
post and can be retried.

## Cover images

A video post can use a frame of the clip or an uploaded image as its cover. The cover is set once on the video and
used on every channel that posts it.

## Checking usage

The account menu shows Storage used and the Plan's amount for an Owner. Billing shows the same figures next to the
Plan.
