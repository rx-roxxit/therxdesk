# Team, members and workspaces

A workspace holds posts, channels and the people who work on them. An Account can hold several workspaces, and one
person can belong to more than one.

## Inviting people

An admin invites a member from Settings, then Members, by email. The invite has an admin switch and a publishing
choice: the member's posts go out directly, or they need approval. The invited person signs in, accepts, and joins
the workspace.

An Invite can be resent to the same address or cancelled from the pending list.

## What a member can do

Members write and schedule posts in the workspace they joined. Approvers, the workspace owner and admins also
decide approval requests. Only the Owner sees Billing, the account's Storage, and the Plan.

A member who needs a channel connected, a setting changed or a Plan change asks a workspace admin or the Owner.

## Workspaces

The Owner creates workspaces and switches between them at the top of the sidebar. Each workspace keeps its own
channels, labels, ideas, templates and members. Posting schedules and queues belong to a channel inside one
workspace.

Deleting a workspace removes its posts and channels. It is a deliberate action, and the app asks the workspace name
before it goes.

## Your profile

Your name, photo, time zone, language and theme are personal settings, not workspace ones. Change them from the
account menu or from Settings, then Profile.
