# Channels

A channel is one connected account on one network, such as a LinkedIn page, an Instagram account or an X profile. A
workspace can hold as many channels as you connect, including several on the same network.

## Connecting a channel

Open Settings, then Channels, and choose Connect. Pick the network and sign in with that account. The app asks only
for what it needs to publish. When the connection finishes, the channel appears in the sidebar and in the composer.

A channel can show a state next to it:

- **Connected** means publishing works.
- **Token expired** or **Disconnected** means the network no longer accepts the saved sign-in. Refresh the
  connection from the channel's card. Posts already scheduled are kept and can be retried after the refresh.

## Channel settings

Each channel has its own settings page. There you can set the posting times that fill the queue, read the channel's
insights, and disconnect it. Disconnecting removes the saved sign-in and stops the channel's queue. It does not
delete posts already published on the network.

## Unavailable networks

A network can be switched off for everyone while it is reviewed or repaired. Its Connect card disappears, and
scheduling a new post on a channel of that network is refused. Channels stay connected and drafts still save. Try
again once the network is back.
