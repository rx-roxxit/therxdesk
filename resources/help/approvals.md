# Approvals

A workspace can require approval before some members' posts publish. A member with approval required writes and
schedules as usual, but the post waits until an approver accepts it.

## Who approves

The workspace owner and every member who publishes directly can approve. A member sees only the approval requests
they made; approvers see all of them in the Approvals tab.

## What a request shows

A pending post shows its content, channel, and the time it is due. Approving schedules or publishes it exactly as
it was sent. Rejecting returns it to the requester as a draft at its requested time.

A member's queued post holds its slot while it waits, so no other post takes that place. If the slot is gone by the
time it is approved, the post moves to the next free slot.

## Changing the rule

Open Settings, then Members. An admin can change a member's approval setting or make them an admin. Admins publish
directly and never need approval.

Approval emails go to the people who need them; each person's notification preferences decide whether an email is
sent.
