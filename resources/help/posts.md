# Posts, scheduling and the queue

A post carries its text, media and one channel. Write it once, then schedule it, publish it now, or keep it as a
draft.

## Writing a post

Open a new post from Posts or from a calendar slot. Choose the channel first: the editor then shows that network's
preview and character limit. Add up to the network's media limits, a cover for a video, labels and notes.

The composer also offers the writing assistant, which suggests or rephrases text. Its suggestions are drafts you
edit before saving.

## Scheduling

Choose a date and time in the workspace's timezone. A scheduled post can be edited, rescheduled, duplicated or
deleted until it publishes. When its time comes, the app sends it and records the result on the post.

A **recurring** post repeats daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or on a custom rhythm until its series ends. Each
occurrence is sent from the same content.

## The queue

A channel's queue posts into the posting times you set on the channel, keeping your chosen slots. Add a post to the
queue with Next or Top, or drag it to a slot. Moving or deleting a queued post leaves its slot free; nothing is
packed forward automatically.

## Calendar and board

The calendar shows every post by day. The board groups posts by status so you can see drafts, scheduled and failed
posts at a glance. A failed post shows the network's error and can be retried.

## Links

A link in a post gets a preview card from its page. On X, links are sent in a non-clickable form to avoid the
network's link treatment; the other networks keep the URL intact.
