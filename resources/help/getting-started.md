# Getting started

The RX Desk writes a post once and publishes it to the channels you connect. Everything lives in a workspace: its
posts, channels, labels, ideas and members.

## Your workspace

You reach the workspace's pages from the sidebar:

- **Posts** lists every post, including drafts, and has the Approvals tab for members whose posts need approval.
- **Calendar** shows scheduled and published posts on a month or week grid.
- **Create** holds the ideas board, templates and feeds.
- **Insights** shows how published posts performed.

The workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar moves between workspaces you belong to. Settings, channels and
members belong to one workspace, so switch the workspace before changing them.

## Your first post

Open Posts and choose New post. Pick a channel, write the post, then choose when it goes out:

- **Draft** keeps it unpublished until you schedule or publish it.
- **Schedule** sends it at the time you pick.
- **Publish now** sends it immediately.

Saving a draft is always allowed, even when a network is unavailable or your Plan's limits are in question.

## Language and theme

Open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Language or Theme. The app remembers both. The
interface language is your own setting; posts always keep the language you write them in.

## If something is missing

- A channel that does not appear in the connect list is not available in your app right now. Ask support for the
  current state.
- A Plan limit, Storage, AI credits and billing live under Billing, which the workspace owner sees.
