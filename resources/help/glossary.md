# Words used in the app

- **Account**: everything an Owner buys and holds: its Plan, Subscription, workspaces and members.
- **Owner**: the person who pays for the Account and holds its Subscription.
- **Member**: a person invited into another Owner's workspace.
- **Workspace**: the place that holds one team's posts, channels, labels, ideas and members.
- **Channel**: one connected account on one network.
- **Post**: one piece of content for one channel.
- **Draft**: a post that is not scheduled or published.
- **Queue**: a channel's upcoming posting slots, filled in the order you choose.
- **Approval**: a post waiting for an approver before it is scheduled or published.
- **Plan**: a monthly or yearly level that sets storage and AI credits. Every feature is the same on every Plan.
- **Subscription**: the recurring payment for the Plan.
- **Trial**: the first days of a Subscription, before the first charge.
- **Complimentary Plan**: a Plan given without a Subscription, usually for a limited time.
- **Lapsed**: an Account whose access has stopped. Its data is deleted after the grace period unless it subscribes
  again.
- **Suspended**: an Account the service has shut off. Its data is kept while the suspension lasts.
- **Storage used**: the size of the media counted toward the Account's Quota.
- **Quota**: the most media an Owner may store, from the Plan.
- **Video policy**: what happens to a video after its post publishes: Keep, Delete or Backup.
- **Backup**: uploading a video to Google Drive, then removing it from our storage.
- **AI credits**: the monthly allowance AI features use.
- **Insight**: a figure a network reports for a published post.
- **Availability**: whether a network or a service is switched on for customers.
- **Support chat**: this assistant.
