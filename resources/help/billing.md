# Billing, Plans and the Trial

An Owner holds one Subscription for one Plan. The Plan sets the storage and the monthly AI credits; every feature is
the same on every Plan.

## The Trial

A new Account starts with a card-required Trial. Nothing is charged until it ends, and the app reminds the Owner
before the first payment. A card that already had a Trial is charged at once instead. One Trial per Account and per
card.

## Paying and renewing

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. Stripe charges the saved card automatically at each renewal and sends the
receipt. An Owner can update the card or read invoices in the Customer Portal from Billing.

## Changing or cancelling

Billing shows the current Plan and a Change plan action. A move to a cheaper Plan or from yearly to monthly can leave
an Account balance, which comes off the next charges; it is never paid back in cash. Cancelling keeps access until
the paid period ends.

A failed payment makes the Account past due. Access pauses until the card is updated, and the app emails the Owner.

## When access stops

If a Subscription ends or a Trial ends unpaid, the Account is lapsed: its data stays for a set number of days and is
then deleted. The deletion date shows in the app and in the emails the app sends. A copy of the data can be requested
before then by contacting support.

## Members and billing

Only the Owner sees Billing. A member's work counts toward the Owner's storage and AI credits. A member who needs a
plan change asks the workspace owner.

Refunds and account exceptions are handled by the support team; the assistant cannot promise them.
