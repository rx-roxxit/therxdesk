# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 17, 2026*

The RX Desk (therxdesk.com) is a private, invite-only tool for scheduling and publishing posts to social networks. It is run by its administrator for a small group of people who join with a signup code or an invite. It is built on [TryPost](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost), and its source code is public at [github.com/rx-roxxit/therxdesk](https://github.com/rx-roxxit/therxdesk).

This policy explains what The RX Desk collects, why, and how you can remove it. Questions go to [admin@therxdesk.com](mailto:admin@therxdesk.com).

## What we collect

- **Your account:** name, email address, a hashed password, and your preferences (language, time zone, time format).
- **What you create:** posts, captions, media you upload or import, schedules, labels, notes and workspace settings.
- **Connected social accounts:** when you connect Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or another network, we store the access tokens that network gives us, plus the account's name, username, profile picture and ID, so we can publish for you and show which account a post goes to.
- **Post results and statistics:** the ID and link of each published post, its publishing status, and the metrics the network reports for posts and accounts you connected (views, likes, comments, followers and similar).
- **Google Drive (optional):** if you turn on Video backup, we store an encrypted refresh token for the Drive access you granted, and the link of each backed-up file.
- **Technical data:** your IP address, browser, and session information, used to keep you signed in and to protect the service from abuse. Server logs are kept for a short time.

We do not use advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

## How we use it

Only to run The RX Desk for you:

- to publish and schedule the posts you ask us to, on the accounts you connected;
- to show your posts, their status and their statistics;
- to back up your videos to your own Google Drive, if you choose that;
- to send you emails about your account (password resets, invites, failed backups and post notifications);
- to keep the service secure and working.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

## Who receives it

- **The social networks you connect** (for example Meta for Facebook and Instagram, Google for YouTube, TikTok): only what is needed to do what you asked, such as the post and its media when it publishes.
- **Google Drive:** your videos, when you turn on Video backup.
- **Our email provider** (Resend): your email address and the content of the emails we send you.
- **AI providers:** only if the administrator turns the writing assistant on, and only the text you send to it.
- **Other members of a workspace** see the posts and channels of that workspace.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

## Google and YouTube

The RX Desk uses YouTube API Services to publish videos and read their statistics. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of your data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can remove The RX Desk's access to your Google account at any time at [security.google.com/settings/security/permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

The RX Desk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive access is limited to the files The RX Desk creates in your Drive.

## Where and how long we keep it

Data is stored on our own server, over encrypted connections (HTTPS), with access tokens encrypted at rest. We keep it while your account exists:

- Media can be removed sooner by the workspace owner's Video policy (deleted after publishing, or moved to their Google Drive).
- Disconnecting a channel deletes its posts from The RX Desk.
- Deleting your account deletes your data from the live service. Copies in the server's backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Posts already published stay on the social network until you delete them there.

## Deleting your data {#data-deletion}

- **Delete your account:** sign in, open **Settings → Profile** and choose **Delete account**. This removes your account and the data listed above from The RX Desk.
- **Disconnect a social account:** open **Channels**, choose the channel and disconnect it. Its tokens and posts are removed.
- **Revoke access from the network's side:** Facebook and Instagram under *Settings & privacy → Apps and websites*, TikTok under *Settings → Security → Manage app permissions*, Google at [security.google.com/settings/security/permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
- **Ask us:** email [admin@therxdesk.com](mailto:admin@therxdesk.com) from your account's address and we will delete your data within 30 days.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it at the same address.

## Children

The RX Desk is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes are announced to members by email.
